<BodySection label="Problem" heading="Every remix that left for an outside distributor took its royalties and its data with it.">
  vers1ons licenses the right to remix. But to get a remix onto Spotify or Apple Music, artists had to take it somewhere else. Outside distributors know nothing about the license chain, so credits came out wrong, the original artist dropped off, and the royalties never came back through us.

  To be worth more as a company, we needed to keep remixes, royalties and data inside vers1ons, and to pay every contributor down a chain of remixes, from the original to the sixth version and beyond.
</BodySection>

<BodySection label="What I did" heading="I owned distribution end to end, from the partner calls to the screens.">
  I worked with our partners to learn how distribution actually works behind the scenes. Then I built our own system around one delivery partner, made so others can plug in: one Inngest function that takes a release from submission to every store, blocking for individual stores that follows each license, and royalty routing that pays everyone in the chain.

  I designed and built the wizard too. Contributors, splits and metadata are hard to explain, so I turned them into steps an artist can get through without knowing any of it.
</BodySection>

<Comparisons />

<BodySection label="Reliability" heading="245+ unit tests plus adversarial endpoint coverage.">
  Metrics, tracing and an ops playbook. Production canaries found real bugs, all fixed: a partner's dedupe conflict, a label and parental-advisory rejection, and codes that didn't issue when the release setup failed.
</BodySection>

<BodySection label="Outcome" heading="Live in production: a remix no longer has to leave vers1ons to reach the stores.">
  Beta users and remix buyers release straight to stores from vers1ons, and labels distribute on behalf of their roster. Every release's credits and splits stay with us, so royalties can follow the chain back to the original artist.

  116 PRs over about ten months: nine checks before any write, seven durable steps, one transaction.
</BodySection>
