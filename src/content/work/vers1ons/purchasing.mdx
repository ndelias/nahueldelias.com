<BodySection label="Problem" heading="Buying a license was harder than it should have been.">
  The listing buried what mattered most. The price sat halfway down the page, and the royalties read like legal text. The checkout itself didn't feel like a place you'd want to spend money. It was hard to follow and didn't look the part. On top of that, checkout was desktop-only. A buyer who found a song on their phone had to go back to the desktop app to finish, and that was a friction point for our users.

  A license isn't a cart item. It comes with terms, an agreement to sign and royalties, and the buyer has to understand all of that before paying. Making it easy couldn't mean hiding any of it.
</BodySection>

<BodySection label="What I did" heading="I redesigned the purchase flow, on desktop and on phones.">
  I redesigned the listing so the song is on one side and the license you're buying is on the other, with the price and Purchase at the top. A purchase bar stays with you as you scroll. Royalties are a short summary, with a worked example one tap away.

  I also redesigned the checkout itself, from the order summary to the agreement and payment. On phones, I took the desktop gate out and rebuilt every step for a small screen. Signing and paying became two steps. You read and sign the agreement first, then payment opens right below it. After buying, the receipt is a small dialog instead of a whole page, and your purchases live in your library with the files and the signed agreement.

  The team built what's behind the screens, like the agreement signing, payments and the rights each purchase grants.
</BodySection>

<BodySection label="Reliability" heading="You can't pay for terms you haven't signed.">
  Payment opens only after the agreement is signed, and each signed agreement keeps its own terms. If an artist changes the price later, licenses already sold don't change. The parts of a live listing that the rest of the platform depends on, like where remixes can be released and the master recording, are locked once it's on sale. The receipt waits for the signed PDF and shows the download as soon as it's ready. When we opened buying to limited accounts, tests covered every closed route, so nothing opened by accident.
</BodySection>

<BodySection label="Outcome" heading="Buying a license now feels as simple as buying a song, on any screen.">
  The listing puts the price, the terms and the royalties up front, where buyers look first. The checkout is clean and easy to follow, from the order summary to the signed agreement. And it works the same on a phone as on desktop, so nobody has to switch devices to finish. What you bought, with the files and the signed agreement, is waiting in your library.
</BodySection>
