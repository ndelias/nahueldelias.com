I started a design lab for the redesign. It runs in development only, at /_lab. Instead of redesigning one screen at a time, I make several layouts of a page, see them side by side as real pages, and keep the parts I like.

- **Round 1 is the homepage:** the design system stays close and the layout varies. There are three layouts next to today's page. Work first puts one sentence about me on the first screen with the work peeking up under it. Lead project features vers1ons with all its numbers and parts. Split keeps me on the left while the work scrolls on the right.
- **The cards show the product:** every card in the lab uses a frame from the project's own reel, not its wordmark, and plays the reel when you point at it.
- **A dial per layer:** each layout records how far it moves from what's live, from a close refinement to an all-new direction, separately for the design system and the layout.
- **Nothing ships:** the lab's route is added only when the dev server runs, and a test checks that no lab page, script, or image reaches the build.
- **Next:** pick what works from each layout, then an editor on the page itself, where a change made on screen is saved back into the code.
