Round 4 in the design lab brings back the lines from my old homepage, three ways, on the new one.

- **The thread:** one line down the gap between my column and the work, drawing as you scroll, with a mark at each section. It ends in an arrow that curves into the meadow's dirt path.
- **The tree:** in About me, lines branch out of one point to each of the five steps and come back together as the section scrolls in. On a phone only the lines coming back together are drawn.
- **The connector:** a line from my photo to whatever you're reading, easing over to the section or project nearest the middle of the screen.
- **On the homepage:** I picked the thread, with a mark at every heading, Education and Leadership included. Once the five steps had descriptions, the tree turned into brackets around them, so I tried three layouts in the lab and picked the simplest. The thread sends a short branch into each step, and nothing rejoins.
- **The five steps say more:** each one now has a short description under its title, so it's clear what I mean by it.
