There's a new page, /motion. It holds small pieces of interaction and motion that run live, and the nav links to it.

- **The first three:** a toggle whose pill rides two springs and stretches on the way, a button you hold to sign, and a dithered noise field drawn by a WebGL shader in the page's own two colors.
- **Labels:** each piece says how it's built and how much code it loads. The size is measured from the built file, so it can't drift.
- **Loading:** a piece's code loads only when its stage comes near the screen, and it pauses off screen. The page loads 1.3 KB of JS up front.
- **Reduced motion:** nothing plays on its own, and the field is one still frame.
- All three are marked Concept, since they were made for this page.
