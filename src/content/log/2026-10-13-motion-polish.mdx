Motion polish from watching the homepage and the vers1ons pages back in recordings.

- **One title at a time:** moving along the strip, the outgoing title clears before the next one fades in. They used to crossfade in the same spot and read as one garbled word for a moment.
- **A shorter glide:** when vers1ons' parts open on the homepage, the card rises less, and the title, recording and parts all move on one timing, so less shifts under the pointer.
- **Parts in the Index:** pointing at one of vers1ons' parts in the Index list shows that part's cover in the preview, as the strip plays that part's recording.
- **An edge in the dark:** in the dark theme, the strip's frames get a hairline, so a dark recording doesn't melt into the dark page.
- **More to scroll to:** on a phone, the row of part tabs fades at the right edge, so a cut-off tab reads as the row going on.
