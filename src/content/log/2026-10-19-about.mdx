The About page is up, told as a path from how I think to how to reach me.

- **A meadow to open it:** a pixel landscape drawn in code from a fixed seed, so it's the same every visit. Clouds drift, cows graze and wander, birds cross, the canopy sways, and scrolling moves its layers at different speeds. It runs only while it's on screen and holds still under reduced motion.
- **How I think about product:** five steps branch out of one point under the headline and come back into my portrait, drawn as you reach them. On a phone they stack, with one line running through them.
- **A portrait in characters:** my photo turned into an 80 by 80 grid of characters once, by a script. Only the grid ships, not the photo. It settles into place as the lines arrive, and flips its shading in the dark theme so it never reads as a negative.
- **A thread to the end:** a line carries on from the statement under the portrait, down my experience with a mark at each role, and ends in an arrow at the ways to reach me. The résumé opens in a new tab or downloads.
- **Two ways in:** my name in the top corner now leads to this page, and the homepage carries the résumé and Contact, small, in its bottom-right corner.
