Wallet & payouts is told in rows now, like Purchasing and Distribution, with a half on how the money works underneath.

- **No bank form to sign up:** payout setup waits until you first sell or distribute.
- **Shares and claims:** every contributor is paid directly, and someone owed money without an account claims it with a code sent to the email they were credited under.
- **Credit:** a giveaway is a real sale, paid from a ledger nobody can edit, that can't be spent twice and hands back the exact credit when a sale dies.
