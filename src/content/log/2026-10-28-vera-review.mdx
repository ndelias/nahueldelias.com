Vera now reviews this site's pull requests, through its first release as a GitHub Action (v0.1.0).

- **One comment per pull request, kept up to date:** the areas a change touches, whether it makes a design decision, and who should look. It reads the change and never approves, blocks or runs it.
- **Who owns what comes from `.vera.yml`:** six areas of this repo, from the homepage to the build checks, with me in each role for now.
