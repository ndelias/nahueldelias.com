Purchasing is the first case study told in rows, as a trial for the other parts.

- **Rows:** each section is a short claim beside its screen, and the sides swap row by row. A heading, a few lines, and the screen that shows it.
- **Shorter:** the page reads in a few minutes now. The draft copy is cut down from the earlier page, with nothing new claimed.
- **The result up top:** the outcome sits with the role and the stack, before the walkthrough.
- **Motion:** each screen swipes in from its side as its row arrives. Point at a screen to look closer. A row's clip plays while it's in view.
- **Still there:** the runnable "How it works", the flow step by step, the phone screens with their viewer, and what changed since the first build. They sit in the same flow.
