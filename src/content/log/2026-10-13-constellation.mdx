The homepage is a constellation now, built for a few projects instead of a strip to scroll.

- **A calm row:** every project stands in one row, the same size, all on screen. With one project, vers1ons stands alone in the middle with its caption.
- **Parts come out of their project:** rest on a project and it comes to the middle, and its parts come out of it, smaller, into a slow orbit around it. The other projects wait in a small row below.
- **One thing forward:** point at a part and it comes forward and plays its recording. The caption says whose part it is.
- **Smooth by construction:** every screen springs toward where it belongs, so opening, switching and closing blend even when you change your mind mid-move. Nothing animates once everything has settled.
- **Same fallbacks:** without JavaScript or with reduced motion, the homepage is still the Index list.
