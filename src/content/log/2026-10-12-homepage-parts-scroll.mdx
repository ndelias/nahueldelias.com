The homepage makes room for vers1ons' parts, and loses some labels it didn't need.

- **Parts you scroll to:** with vers1ons focused, its five parts run one under the other below the frame, each with its cover, number, title and line, and the page scrolls down to them. vers1ons lifts a little, and the foot of the screen fades over the next part, so it reads as more below.
- **Out of the way:** pointing at the parts, tabbing into them or scrolling clears the fade and hides Prev and Next. They come back at the top. On any other project the strip sits where it always did.
- **Fewer labels:** the 01 / 08 counter is gone, so are the W01 and P01 tags on the strip's frames, and the version and date over each image in a case study's Versions stack. The tabs beside the stack still name each version.
