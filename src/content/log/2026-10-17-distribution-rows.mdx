Distribution is told in rows now, with the result up top and a half on how the system underneath works.

- **Start from the license:** a release starts from the track, and its credits arrive locked from the signed split sheet.
- **A job that refuses first:** store codes are permanent, so delivery checks everything it needs before minting any, reuses what a first run made, and only retries what a retry can fix.
- **Live status:** each milestone is saved before it's announced, so a reload never shows a stale step.
- **Stores and royalties:** a license that excludes stores drops them and ships the rest, and a contributor without an account has their share held until they claim it.
- **Failures:** a failed release shows where it stopped, the notice actually arrives, and the artist resubmits without starting over.
