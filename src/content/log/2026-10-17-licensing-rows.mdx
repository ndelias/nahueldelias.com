Licensing's three tabs are told in rows now, each with a half on how it works underneath.

- **Listings:** contributors and their splits in one list, one rule that decides where a remix may release, and what locks once a listing is live.
- **Drops:** agreeing to the rules is recorded as evidence, stems only reach buyers, and the windows are enforced where a sale becomes real.
- **Sheets:** nothing is saved until you ask, every signature binds to the version that was sent, and a signed sheet arrives in vers1ons as a draft listing.
