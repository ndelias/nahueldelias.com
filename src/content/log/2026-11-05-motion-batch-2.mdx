/motion has six more pieces, the functional ones that help someone through a product. That makes 15 of 30.

- **Number ticker:** digits roll on their own columns, the ones first.
- **Copy button:** the icon becomes a check, and a failed copy says so honestly.
- **Toast stack:** notices tuck behind each other, spread out on hover, and swipe away.
- **Drag to reorder:** the others glide aside, and it works from the keyboard too.
- **Swipe to delete:** the row goes past a line, the space closes, and Undo brings it back.
- **Accordion:** CSS alone, on native details.
- **A fix on the way:** a stage's styles were scoped to its whole piece, so the accordion's bare `p` reached the caption under it and hid it. They're scoped to the stage now.
