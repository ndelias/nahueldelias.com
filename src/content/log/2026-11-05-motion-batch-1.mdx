/motion has five more pieces, the first of a batch toward thirty, and it now loads in pages.

- **From vers1ons, shipped:** read to sign (the license reader that unlocks signing at the end), the split balancer from Sheets (no share can push the total past 100%), and the drop page's screen swap, here in a box of its own.
- **From this site:** the About portrait's scramble and settle, and the springs behind /work's constellation with the dials out.
- **Loading in pages:** at about 0.7 KB of HTML a piece, thirty won't fit in one page's budget. /motion holds the first four; /motion/2/ onward hold the rest, and they're fetched and appended as the end nears. Without JS they're plain links.
- **Styles travel with their piece:** each stage's CSS is inlined beside its markup and scoped to it, so a page carries only the styles it shows.
