Distribution is the first part with its real content: the recordings, the system behind it, and how it changed between the first build and today.

- **Real media, without the demo cursor.** An import script brings picked screens and recordings in from the archive outside the repo. It refuses anything that looks like a secret, and it finds the capture rig's cursor and patches it out from a frame of the same recording where that spot is clean. Every retouch gets a before/after crop to check. Where a capture caught the cover art still loading, the screen gets the finished artwork it was loading.
- **The flow, step by step.** Fourteen screens on a sideways track, one at a time, with short clips where something moves. They advance on their own while in view: a still for five seconds, a clip once through. Pointing at the screens or tabbing into the section holds them, and Pause stops them. Under reduced motion nothing moves and no clip loads.
- **What changed, and why.** Five before/now pairs, each with the reason. Today's screen fades in over the early one in the same frame, then the next pair comes up. Under reduced motion they sit side by side.
- **The parts row rolls up.** At rest the parts above the header are one quiet row of numbers and titles; pointing at it lowers their thumbnails like a blind, moving the page down with it, and it waits a moment before rolling back up.
- **The system, runnable.** The diagram of how one click reaches every store runs a release, a partner timeout or a refused license on press, as real text. The run buttons, the step-by-step log and the inspector sit in the margin beside it, under the caption; the log grows a line per step, up to the diagram's height.
- **The walkthrough is centered on eight columns,** looping. A screen recording shown wider than it was captured only gets softer.
- **Everything below the fold loads after the first paint.** Its styles and behaviours arrive once the largest content has painted, so each page's HTML stays inside TCP's first round trip: Distribution's is 13.1KB, with LCP at 1.05s.
- **A link to a section lands on it** even before the deferred styles arrive. Once they apply, the page renders the skipped blocks for one frame so they remember their real heights, then lands again, unless the reader has scrolled.
