vers1ons has a page for its money, laid out like a finance app, at /work/vers1ons/money, featured on the vers1ons page in a card above the parts, and on /work with vers1ons' parts: a sixth card in the orbit that plays the ledger, and a line in the Index, both as W01.$.

- **Everyone who gets paid:** one license sale, from checkout to the split, out to every person with a share, and on to the royalties once the remix is released.
- **One purchase, in the ledger:** a small working model. Pick how much credit the buyer has and buy, and the entries post in signed cents, the sale settles once, a second spend is refused, and a declined card hands the credit back.
- **Why it can't spend twice:** the eight rules that keep the ledger exact, and how contributors without accounts get paid, verified by email and by text.
- **Framer Motion, lightly:** the map and the ledger run on Preact with Framer Motion's smaller build, about 54KB on this page and none anywhere else. Everything reads without it.
