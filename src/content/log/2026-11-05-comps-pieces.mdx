I built the three live pieces for a new concept study, a comps builder where every number shows the filing it came from.

- **The fill:** the table fills itself row by row once it's on screen. A number nothing could be traced to stays blank and asks for the analyst.
- **The trace and the correction:** pick a number to open its source beside the table, or under the row on a phone. Fix one, and the multiples, median, and mean count to their new values.
- **Next:** the real numbers, from Toast's and its peers' Q3 2026 filings. The pieces run on sample numbers until then, and the page stays unlisted.
