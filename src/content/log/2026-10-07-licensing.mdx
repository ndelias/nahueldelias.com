Licensing is the second part with real content, and the first told in tabs: listings, drops and split sheets each have their own walkthrough, steps and before/now.

- **Tabs that switch in place, without a heavier page.** Each tab is its own page, with only that tab's content in its HTML, so all three stay inside TCP's first round trip (12.7KB for the first). The row is plain links. Once the page is idle the other tabs are fetched, and choosing one swaps the content below the row, puts its URL in the address bar and keeps Back and Forward working. Without JS the links just load the page.
- **What comes in is set up.** The steps, comparisons, gallery and walkthrough behaviours pick up content a tab brings in, and each piece is set up once.
- **Each part works differently, so each tab has its own runnable "How it works":** a listing from upload to royalties and round again (with a remix of a remix, and a store the license doesn't allow), a drop where no one leaves a loser, and the split sheet app that brings artists in. Above the tabs: only the decision and the problem.
- **Real media from the archive,** imported the same way as Distribution's: three walkthroughs, eight step clips, 31 screens and seven before/now pairs.
