Wallet & payouts is the third part with its real content, on one through line: money follows the rights.

- **One walkthrough, one sale:** a license bought, the artist's share in his wallet, a contributor with no account held and invited, then claiming it by email. It stops where her payouts wait to be set up.
- **Feature by feature:** nine small features on one track (the wallet, withdrawing, every contributor's share, how royalties work, claiming by email, bank details later, credit and card, covered by credit, earnings by song), with clips where something moves.
- **A runnable "Money follows the rights":** a sale split by the license to every rights holder, a contributor without an account, a purchase paid with credit, and a credit sale that never completes.
- **Stills from recordings:** import-media can cut a frame from a recording, and copy a word back from its twin line when the cursor sat on it.
