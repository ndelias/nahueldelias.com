/motion is a sheet now, not a list. Every piece had the same box in two even columns, so a copy button sat in as much grey as the dither field.

- **A tile per piece, sized to what it holds:** a size of columns x rows on a 12-column grid, so the field and the tape get room and a toggle gets a strip. The name and badge sit at the top of the tile, the note and how it's built at the bottom.
- **The header is the top left tile,** its headline level with the bottom of the two pieces beside it.
- **Rows have to fill:** the build places the tiles the way the grid does and fails on a gap, naming the piece after it. Only the last row may be short.
- **The portrait doesn't happen just once:** it settles again each time it comes back into view, and the cells under the pointer scramble and settle back.
- **Tablet and phone:** two columns, then one, with heights from the stage. On a phone a one-row piece gets a shorter stage.
- **Hold to sign follows the clock:** with the field now playing beside it on the first screen, a slow machine drew fewer frames and the fill moved by frames, so a 1.2s hold could take longer. It moves by elapsed time now, and letting go counts the time since the last frame.
