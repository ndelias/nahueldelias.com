Vera's comment on this site's pull requests is redesigned (v0.3.0).

- **Action first:** a table of who has to sign off and who has, kept current as people sign off.
- **Where to look first:** the 3 to 5 places that most need a person's judgment, each linked to its lines and naming who should check it. On 105 real pull requests, these were the files reviewers commented on [71% of the time, against 64% for "biggest files first"](https://vera-agent.vercel.app/evals).
- **How it decided** is folded away below, for when you want the reasoning.
