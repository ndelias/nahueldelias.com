vers1ons' five parts are now one step away from the homepage and from the hub.

- **On the strip:** while vers1ons is focused, its parts come down under the frame as a list, from W01.1 Design system to W01.5 Distribution, each linked to its page. vers1ons stays the focused frame while you point at them, and the list folds back up when you move to another project. On shorter screens the list runs top to bottom in two columns, and the strip sits a little higher so the list clears Prev and Next.
- **In the Index:** the parts are rows under vers1ons. They come down while it's the active row and stay down while the pointer is in the list, so the rows below never jump out from under it. Without JavaScript they're always shown.
- **On the hub:** the parts row from the part pages now sits on top of vers1ons too, with thumbnails that drop down on hover and the hub's header under an ink rule.
