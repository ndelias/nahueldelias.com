Vera's review on this site's pull requests now comes with a sign-off check (v0.2.0).

- **A "Vera: sign-off" check on each pull request:** who Vera asked, and who has signed off. It's grey while someone still has to, green once everyone has, and never blocks a merge.
- **Signing off is an approval or a comment:** `/vera sign-off` works on my own pull requests, which GitHub won't let me approve. Neither calls the model, so it costs nothing.
